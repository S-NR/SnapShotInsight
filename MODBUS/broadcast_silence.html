Notes:
  • Modbus RTU convention uses 3.5 character times as the frame separator. Character time = bits-per-character / baud.
  • The calculator uses your chosen bits-per-character (commonly 10 for 8N1).
  • For broadcast (ID=0), masters typically wait this silence to ensure no slave responds or to let slaves finish local actions.

Examples

Baud 9600, 8N1 (10 bits): 1 char = 10/9600 = 1.0417 ms → 3.5 char ≈ 3.65 ms
Baud 19200, 8N1: 1 char = 0.5208 ms → 3.5 char ≈ 1.82 ms
Baud 115200, 8N1: 1 char ≈ 0.0868 ms → 3.5 char ≈ 0.304 ms
