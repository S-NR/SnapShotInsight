Understanding USB Eye Diagrams

  • An eye diagram shows the quality of a high-speed digital signal by overlapping many bits of data.
  • The waveform creates a shape that looks like an “eye”.
  • The central opening is the Sampling Window, where the data receiver decides 1 or 0.

PASS eye

PASS Eye Characteristics

  • Large vertical opening → strong signal amplitude.
  • Large horizontal opening → stable timing & low jitter.
  • Waveform does not enter the compliance mask region.
  • Receiver easily distinguishes 1s and 0s → low bit error rate (BER).

FAIL eye

FAIL Eye Characteristics

  • Reduced vertical & horizontal opening → collapsed eye.
  • Waveforms overlap and touch the mask boundary.
  • Hard to determine 1s vs 0s → high bit error rate.
  • Indicates poor signal integrity or design issues.

Typical Causes for Eye Collapse

  • Impedance mismatch / reflections on PCB traces.
  • Weak driver amplitude or unstable power.
  • Clock jitter or noisy environment.
  • Crosstalk from nearby high-speed signals.

Summary

  • PASS = open eye = clean signal = reliable USB link.
  • FAIL = closed eye = distorted signal = communication failure.
