USB 2.0 Eye Diagram — I, J & K Compliance Test Results

The I, J, K tests are standard USB 2.0 High-Speed electrical compliance tests. They verify that the PHY output signal meets required timing, jitter and amplitude specifications under controlled conditions.

When thousands of bits are overlaid on the oscilloscope, a shape resembling an “eye” is formed. A valid USB signal produces a large open eye window, allowing the receiver to sample correctly.

The highlighted dashed box in each waveform is the Sampling Window where the receiver makes its decision between logic 1 or 0. If waveforms intrude into this window or collapse, the test fails.

USB Eye Diagram Test Results — I / J / K Test

PASS — Healthy Eye

Clear vertical & horizontal opening — no mask hits.

  • Eye opening is wide and clearly visible.
  • No waveform touches the mask region.
  • Receiver easily distinguishes 1s and 0s.
  • Amplitude and jitter are within specification.
  • Represents a healthy USB link with low BER.

FAIL I — Overall Eye Collapse

Both vertical & horizontal margins collapse — waveform enters mask from all sides.

  • Eye height and width both reduced.
  • Signal overlaps mask on multiple boundaries.
  • Caused by severe signal integrity problems.
  • Receivers fail to distinguish levels and timing → link unstable or dropouts.

FAIL J — Timing / Jitter

Horizontal eye width reduced — edges jitter into mask timing area.

  • Eye is horizontally compressed.
  • Waveforms overlap sampling window boundary.
  • Indicates timing jitter or clock instability.
  • High probability of sampling errors → incorrect bits captured.

FAIL K — Amplitude Collapse

Vertical eye height reduced — voltage margin insufficient (touches mask).

  • Eye height is reduced → weak amplitude.
  • Signal crosses into forbidden mask region.
  • Can be caused by reflections, impedance mismatch, bad cable or connector.
  • Receiver cannot reliably separate 1s and 0s.

Summary

  • PASS → Clear open eye → Signal integrity OK
  • FAIL-I → Inter-Symbol Interference (ISI) → Both horizontal & vertical closure,
  • FAIL-J → Timing jitter → Horizontal closure
  • FAIL-K → Amplitude issue / noise → Vertical collapse
