SD Card Heating — Quick Troubleshoot

If an SD card heats immediately on connecting — treat it as a fault. Follow the checks below.

Summary

SD cards should feel warm during activity, but not hot within seconds. Immediate heating usually signals an electrical fault — stop using the card and follow safe debugging steps below.

Common Causes

  • Internal short or controller failure — card draws excessive current and heats fast.
  • Damaged/bent contacts — bridged pins can short VCC to other lines.
  • Faulty reader/adapter — reader supplies wrong voltage or shorts pins.
  • Wrong board power (embedded) — 5V supplied instead of 3.3V or regulator issue.
  • Counterfeit / poor-quality card — internal defects lead to overheating.

Immediate Actions (do this first)

  1. Unplug the card immediately if it becomes hot in 3–5 seconds.
  2. Do not insert into expensive devices (phone/laptop) again.
  3. Try a different reader/PC — if it only heats in one reader, the reader is faulty.
  4. If it heats everywhere, assume the card is dead and replace it.

Diagnostic Checklist

Step How to Expected / Action
Visual inspect Check contacts for burns, debris, or bending. If damaged → do not reuse; replace.
Swap reader Test with another USB reader or different PC. Heats only in one reader → replace reader.
Check board voltage (embedded) Measure VDD at SD slot: should be ~3.3V. Wrong voltage → fix regulator; do not use card.
Current draw Use an ammeter in series; normal idle <100 mA. High draw → shorted card or reader.

What NOT to do

Don't keep reinserting — repeated attempts can damage the host slot. Don't attempt reformat/repair while it still overheats.
