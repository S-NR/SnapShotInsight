Quick Reference

Big Endian: store most-significant byte at lowest address. Example 0x12345678 → [12][34][56][78]

Little Endian: store least-significant byte at lowest address. Example 0x12345678 → [78][56][34][12]

Modbus RTU: registers are transmitted as High byte, Low byte per 16-bit register. For 32-bit values split across two registers, vendor/device manuals decide whether the high 16-bit word comes first or second — so test and swap accordingly.
