Manufacturer obligations & reporting

Designing a secure product is only half of the CRA. The manufacturer must also support it, report incidents, document it and inform users (Art. 13–14, 28–31).

Reporting (Art. 14): applies since 11 Sep 2026

Reports go through the ENISA single reporting platform to the coordinator CSIRT. This applies to all products on the EU market, including units sold before 2027.

Minimal reporting runbook

Intake

Published security contact (e.g. security@…) and CVD policy. Every report gets an owner and a timestamp.

Triage (hours)

Which products and versions are affected? Use the SBOM per release. Is it actively exploited?

Notify (≤ 24 h / 72 h)

Pre-registered ENISA platform account, prepared templates, named backup person.

Fix & inform

Security-only release, free of charge, advisory to customers, final report within 14 days of the fix.

User information (Annex II)

  • Manufacturer name, address, contact
  • Single point of contact for vulnerabilities and the CVD policy location
  • Product name, type and identification
  • Intended purpose and security environment
  • Known circumstances that may lead to significant risk
  • Where to find the EU declaration of conformity
  • Type of support and end date of the support period
  • Secure commissioning / installation instructions
  • How security updates are installed (and how to postpone or turn off automatic updates)
  • Secure decommissioning and data removal
  • Where the SBOM can be accessed, if it is made available

For EX-100: add support end date, network segmentation guidance for Modbus TCP and M-Bus, how to verify firmware authenticity, how to set the PIN, and how to erase MRAM/SD at decommissioning.
