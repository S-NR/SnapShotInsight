Five things to remember

It is a product law

Anything with firmware and a data connection sold in the EU is in scope, wherever it was engineered.

Most of our products are "default" class

Self-assessment (Module A) is available. Network/security devices are "important", and smart meter gateways are "critical".

Reporting already applies

Since 11 Sep 2026: actively exploited vulnerabilities must be reported within 24 h / 72 h / 14 days, for products already in the field too.

Full compliance from 11 Dec 2027

Secure/signed updates, no default passwords, access control on configuration, SBOM, CVD policy, support period of at least 5 years.

Medical devices and type-approved vehicles are excluded

Their own sector laws (MDR/IVDR, UNECE R155/R156) apply instead.
