Systec products & the CRA

systec Controls (Germany) manufactures and sells measurement instruments under its own name in the EU, so Systec is the CRA manufacturer. Tismo, as firmware engineering partner, delivers much of the evidence. Its customers (water, power, district heating, gas, chemicals, pharma) are often NIS2 entities and will ask for cybersecurity evidence anyway.

Recommended Systec roadmap

Product inventory & classification (now)

Every product and software tool: interfaces, firmware version, MCU, components, EU sales after Dec 2027.

Vulnerability-handling process (now, legally required)

Security contact, CVD policy, Art. 14 runbook, ENISA platform account, internal owner.

SBOM recovery & CVE baseline

For each firmware and PC tool. DWC2 is the pilot.

Per-product risk assessment

Annex I.1, with IEC 62443-4-2 as checklist. DWC2 Phase A/B is the template.

Security retrofit

Signed updates → config write protection → debug lock → data integrity → security log → secure reset.

Technical file, DoC, Annex II

Per product, including support end date and secure installation guidance.

Support period & end-of-sale decisions

Decide which older models get retrofitted and which stop selling before Dec 2027.

Why DWC2 is the baseline

A representative legacy instrumentDWC2 combines every typical Systec risk point: field buses, USB, SD-card updates, local UI, calibration data and a long field life, on a resource-tight MCU without crypto hardware.
