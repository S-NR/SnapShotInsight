Risk-assessment method & templates

Annex I Part I point 1 asks for security "appropriate to the risks", based on a documented cybersecurity risk assessment that becomes part of the technical file (Annex VII). The team method has two parts: a guideline (how to run the assessment) and a template set T1–T15 (what to record). EX-100 is the first product assessed with it.

Sources: · (v1.2)

The process: ISO 31000 in 8 steps, with STRIDE

1 · Scope & deployment

Product, essential functions, environment, users, personal data, support period (T1).

2 · Classify

CRA scope, operator role, product class and route; component security levels (T2, T3).

3 · Risk criteria

Likelihood and impact scales, bands, who approves what; frozen before rating (T4).

4 · Identify

Assets, interfaces, data flows and trust boundaries, STRIDE threats, supplier components (T5–T9).

5–6 · Analyse & evaluate

Existing controls, L × I, band, assumptions and confidence, decision (T10).

7 · Treat

Controls with requirement IDs and Annex I references; Part I/II coverage; traceability to tests; residual-risk sign-off and gate checklist (T11–T14).

8 · Record & review

Every trigger (design change, new vulnerability, incident) reopens the record; Art. 14 reportability checked (T15).

The guideline's worked example is a connected thermostat. ID scheme: A- assets · IF- interfaces · DF-/TB- flows and boundaries · C- components · TH- threats · R- risks · REQ-SEC- requirements · TC- tests.

Default risk criteria (T4 example, 5 × 5)

Likelihood

1 rare (lab equipment, expert) · 2 unlikely (physical access + specialist) · 3 possible (radio/network range, public tools) · 4 likely (remote, known technique) · 5 almost certain (remote, automated, exploit published)

Impact

1 negligible · 2 minor (one device, self-recovers) · 3 moderate (one user's data or function lost) · 4 major (safety-relevant or many users) · 5 severe (fleet-wide, physical harm, attacks other systems)

Bands

16–25 Critical release blocked · 10–15 High treat before release · 5–9 Medium treat or retain with signature · 1–4 Low accept and record

Adapt the wording per product line and get it approved before rating. EX-100 uses an industrial adaptation (see S4), pending approval (Q-13).

Review verdict (23 Nov 2026)

Not perfect, but a solid baseThe templates follow the guideline's process one-to-one with a clean ID scheme. The gaps were CRA obligations that sit outside a pure ISO 31000 process: Annex I Part II, Art. 14 reporting, Annex II user information and the economic-operator role. 20 of 25 findings were fixed in v1.2 (26 → 29 slides).
