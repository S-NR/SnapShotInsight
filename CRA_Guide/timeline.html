Units already sold

Always

Reporting still applies

Art. 14 reporting covers every product on the EU market, old units included.

From 11 Dec 2027

Each new unit

"Placing on the market" applies per unit. A model launched in 2015 but still sold in 2028 must comply for every unit sold after the date.

Substantial modificationA firmware release that changes security-relevant behaviour, or adds functions beyond the original risk assessment, can count as substantial. Security fixes alone normally do not. Plan each EX-100 release with this in mind.
