What changed from the first matrix (12 Nov 2026)

The first matrix used a 4×4 scale (R01 Critical, seven High). Draft 0.2 uses the 5×5 criteria of the templates (pending approval, Q-13) and IEC 31010 rating rules. Changes: R-02 6→8 (IP loss is Major), R-06 8→9 (crash path needs no skill), and four new risks R-16–R-19 for security logging, secure erase, the missing SAST gate and bus behaviour. The 4×4 values remain in the internal action plan.

Risk criteria (T4, EX-100 proposal)

Likelihood 1–5

1 lab equipment, opened housing, expert · 2 physical access + specialist skills · 3 field bus / plant network, or physical access with public tools · 4 remote, known technique · 5 remote, automated, exploit published.

Impact 1–5

1 cosmetic · 2 one device's readout degraded, recovers · 3 one device's measurement or config lost, site visit · 4 falsified measurement or billing, plant affected, IP lost · 5 many sites, safety-relevant, attacks other systems.

Risk = L × I

1–4 Low 5–9 Medium 10–15 High 16–25 Critical

Critical blocks release · High must be treated before release · Medium treated or retained with a signed decision · Low accepted and recorded. Rules (IEC 31010): one consequence per risk, assumptions and confidence recorded; L × I ranks risks, it does not measure them. Approval pending (Q-13).

Review status of draft 0.2

Verified on 24 Nov 2026

All L × I scores and bands, the before-treatment counts, the roadmap coverage (all 19 risks) and the REQ-SEC-01–16 references are consistent across the deck.

Open points before sending to the product manager

1. The cover notes reference EX-100_Review_v0.1_vs_Project_Context.md and a "case study v0.1", which are not in the CRA folder.

2. "After treatment: 4 Medium / 15 Low" has residual ratings for only 6 risks; rate the rest or label the column as a target.

3. R-06 combines two consequences (crash 3×3, code execution 2×4), against the "one consequence per risk" rule.

4. The T4 rule "likelihood per device over the support period" does not match the access-based likelihood scale.

5. T4 should state that a known exploitable vulnerability blocks release regardless of band (R-06).
