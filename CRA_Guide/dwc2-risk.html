Risk method

Likelihood 1–4

1 rare · 2 possible · 3 likely · 4 almost certain. Considers attacker access, effort and knowledge.

Impact 1–4

1 minor · 2 moderate · 3 major · 4 severe. Considers measurement integrity, plant operation, billing, IP and reputation.

Risk = L × I

1–3 low 4–6 medium 8–9 high 12–16 critical

Aligned with ISO/IEC 27005 / IEC 62443-3-2 style analysis. Final method to confirm with the client (Q-13).
