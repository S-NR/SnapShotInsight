IMPORTANT: VIN/E5V powering path behaves differently from ST-LINK USB power path.

Most Probable Root Causes

1. Clock Startup Instability PLL/Clock configuration may become unstable during cold boot when external power ramps differently. ST-LINK USB often provides cleaner startup timing.
2. Power Ramp Timing Issue External supply rise time may be slower/faster causing improper PLL lock or peripheral initialization failure.
3. HSE Oscillator Startup Failure Ethernet RMII depends heavily on clock stability. HSE startup timing may fail only during external-only power boot.
4. Voltage Drop During Boot External supply may dip briefly during initialization causing brownout-like behaviour.
5. ST-LINK Side Effects ST-LINK connection may unintentionally stabilize NRST, SWD, ground reference, or startup sequencing.

Why Ethernet Can Be Affected

  • Ethernet RMII requires precise clock timing.
  • PLL instability can prevent ETH MAC startup.
  • NetX waits for Ethernet driver readiness.
  • Cold boot instability may block IP initialization.
  • PHY reset timing may fail during external-only boot.

Recommended Investigation Order

STEP 1 Reduce system clock frequency temporarily
STEP 2 Disable PLL2 / PLL3 temporarily
STEP 3 Test using HSI instead of HSE
STEP 4 Disable Ethernet temporarily
STEP 5 Add startup delay before Ethernet init
STEP 6 Verify NRST behaviour during cold boot

Key Technical Insight

Since the board:

  • Works after ST-LINK-assisted startup
  • Continues running after cable removal
  • Fails only during cold external boot

the issue is likely NOT firmware logic failure, but rather:

Power sequencing, clock startup, or reset timing instability during cold boot.
